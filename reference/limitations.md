# Limitations

- The guide cannot guarantee deliverability, reputation, or provider acceptance.
- It is not a complete configuration generator and does not replace testing against installed versions.
- It does not provide legal, privacy, records-retention, or regulatory advice.
- High availability, multi-datacenter routing, and very high-volume bulk mail are outside the 1.0 reference design.
- Antivirus engines, commercial reputation feeds, identity federation, and MFA gateways are deployment-specific.
- DNS examples do not include DNSSEC/DANE deployment; those are roadmap items.
- Read-only scripts cannot prove mailbox integrity, business readiness, or absence of compromise.
- External testing can be affected by NAT, split DNS, firewalls, rate limits, and provider policy.
